# Open questions and decisions taken (conservative interpretation, recorded per AGENTS.md)

1. **Time vs tier.** The master prompt defaults to Compact (10–12 h). The real time left was about 3 h, so Core+ was built (see PLAN.md). Phase 4 items not built are listed in CUT_LOG.md.
2. **FileStore format.** The blueprint says Parquet. Facts are stored as pandas pickles (`data/store/<seed>/*.pkl`) to avoid a pyarrow dependency. Mutable state uses sqlite3 as specified. PostgresStore is not built, so **do not claim PostgreSQL/pgvector in the pitch**.
3. **Investigate is plain JSON, not SSE.** `POST /incidents/{id}/investigate` returns the full trace with per-step timestamps; the UI reveals the steps in sequence. The steps are deterministic and take milliseconds in keyless mode.
4. **SKU-scope attach rule (interpretation of `source_counting`).** SKU signals (stock cover, ETA slips) attach to an account incident only when (a) the account shows adverse signals from at least 2 of its own source systems **and** (b) its own fill rate on that SKU over the last 4 weeks is < 0.90, meaning the shortage reached it. Other buyers of the SKU are listed in the Blast Radius. Without this rule, every buyer of a short SKU with one weak random signal became a false incident.
5. **Keyless cause confidence.** = 0.6 × weighted fraction of rule conditions met (requires 1.0, supports 0.5) + 0.4 × share of the incident's adverse evidence weight that the rule explains. The plain "fraction met" ranked "field coverage gap" above "supplier delay" for the hero, because a touchpoint drop alone satisfied that rule fully. Tuned on seed A only.
6. **Hero designed counts.** For the hero account, the generator sets the last-4-week complaint and touchpoint totals to `round(multiplier × median of the account's own pre-onset 4-week totals)` (designed scenario, see the scenarios.yaml note). Order and response-time effects are multipliers on noisy draws. The engine recovers all four effects from rows; nothing is hard-coded in the engine or UI.
7. **Grouped incidents.** Region (S10) and rep-vacancy (S08) incidents are created by rule when ≥ 5 accounts in the group show the signal. Their score is the noisy-OR of the group's witnesses, capped by the single/two-source rule.
8. **Regulatory incidents** get display severity `high` regardless of score (banding is bypassed per the catalog's hard rule); the risk score shown is still the computed noisy-OR value.
9. **API types** in `apps/web/src/lib/api/types.ts` are hand-written from docs/API.md; generating them with `openapi-typescript` is a follow-up.
10. **Persistence bonus (+0.05)** is not implemented: it needs a second weekly evaluation run. Scores are therefore slightly conservative.
11. **Approval roles.** A plan can be approved by its `requires_role`, or by an Operations Manager or Business Head, except QA-routed plans, which only `qa_head` can approve (two different names for four-eyes).
12. **Ask the mentor**: the Blueprint §1.1 questions are still open (internal tools, a rupee figure for a lost stockist, the real manual-review time).
